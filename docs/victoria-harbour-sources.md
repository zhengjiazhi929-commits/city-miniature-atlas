# Victoria Harbour miniature — sources and scope

Checked 2026-09-08. Original procedural geometry; no external photographs,
building models, logos or textures are included in the distributable.

- [Hong Kong Tourism Board — Hong Kong Convention and Exhibition Centre](https://www.discoverhongkong.com/eng/place-to-go/travel.guide-hong-kong-convention-and-exhibition-centre.html): Wan Chai location, glazed exterior and aluminium roof conceived as a flying seabird guide the waterfront hall and curved shell.
- [Bank of China (Hong Kong) — About BOC Tower](https://www.bochk.com/m/en/aboutus/corpprofile/boctower.html): Central location, four prismatic shafts and twin masts guide the distinct stepped triangular tower, rather than generic rectangular massing.
- [IFC operator — One & Two ifc](https://ifc.com.hk/en/office/one-two-ifc/): Central location and finial crown guide the tapered rounded tower and raised roof fins.
- [Hong Kong Tourism Board — Central Plaza](https://www.discoverhongkong.com/eng/place-to-go/travel.guide-central-plaza.html): triangular plan guides the Wan Chai tower; the miniature retains its spire.
- [Hong Kong Tourism Board — Tsim Sha Tsui Promenade](https://www.discoverhongkong.com/eng/place-to-go/travel.guide-tsim-sha-tsui-promenade.html): Kowloon waterfront, clock tower and Star Ferry pier stand across the harbour from Hong Kong Island.
- [Star Ferry operator — Company Information](https://www.starferry.com.hk/tc/theCompany): double-ended traditional ferries, white upper hull and green lower hull inform the small animated boats.

World-space orientation: Kowloon is at positive z (north/foreground), Hong Kong
Island at negative z (south/background), and east increases with x. Central's IFC
is west of the Bank of China Tower and Wan Chai's Convention Centre. This retains
the essential shoreline relationship, but the exact coastline, topography,
street blocks, setbacks, building heights relative to one another, and distances
are deliberately compressed. Contextual buildings are illustrative.

The model is a scenic miniature, not a current-condition survey. In particular,
[HKTB's Clock Tower page](https://www.discoverhongkong.com/eng/place-to-go/travel.guide-clock-tower.html)
currently reports maintenance scaffolding through late 2026. This artwork shows
the clock tower's landmark appearance without temporary construction coverings.

The ferry animation is a gentle representative crossing within the miniature,
not a live vessel location or an exact navigational route.
