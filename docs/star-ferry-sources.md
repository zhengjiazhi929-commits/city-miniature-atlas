# Star Ferry miniature — sources and scope

Checked 2026-09-08. This is a separate close-up composition focused on the boat,
using original editable geometry. No photographs, branding assets or third-party
models are redistributed.

- [Star Ferry operator — 公司簡介](https://www.starferry.com.hk/tc/theCompany): traditional double-ended design, white upper and green lower hull, funnel stars and cross-harbour routes inform the model's silhouette, colors and deck arrangement. The operator page's [vessel and pier photograph](https://www.starferry.com.hk/sites/default/files/deco/dummy_unsplash_zRnU-14t4Bw.png) was also viewed in the browser to check two-deck window rhythm and green/white color placement.
- [Star Ferry operator — Company Information](https://www.starferry.com.hk/en/theCompany): ferry service connects Kowloon with Central and Wan Chai. The modern Central Pier No. 7 has its own clock tower; it is distinct from the red-brick Tsim Sha Tsui Clock Tower modeled in this foreground.
- [Hong Kong Tourism Board — Tsim Sha Tsui Promenade](https://www.discoverhongkong.com/eng/place-to-go/travel.guide-tsim-sha-tsui-promenade.html): waterfront relation between the Star Ferry pier, Clock Tower and views across Victoria Harbour.
- [Hong Kong Tourism Board — Clock Tower](https://www.discoverhongkong.com/eng/place-to-go/travel.guide-clock-tower.html): red brick and granite guide the adjoining landmark. The model omits temporary maintenance scaffolding currently described by HKTB.

The hero ferry is intentionally enlarged against the pier and distant skyline to
make windows, railings, deck levels, lifebuoys and the two bridge ends readable.
It represents the familiar traditional fleet design, not a survey of one named
vessel. The far skyline is compressed; boat bobbing is illustrative and does not
simulate harbour physics. See [harbour references](./victoria-harbour-sources.md)
for the distant IFC and Bank of China Tower geometry.
