# West Lake miniature references

Both scenes are original procedural Three.js geometry. No reference photograph,
third-party model, texture or tourism logo is redistributed. The scenes compress
distances and scale small landmarks up for legibility; they are artistic miniature
compositions, **not surveyed reconstructions, navigation maps or site plans**.

Verified on 2026-09-08:

1. [Hangzhou government — Three Pools Mirroring the Moon](https://www.ehangzhou.gov.cn/2018-06/14/c_271616.htm)
   identifies three small stone pagodas, five circular apertures on each, and
   the island's pavilions and curved bridges. Used for the lantern and garden details.
2. [Zhejiang government — Three Pools Mirroring the Moon](https://www.ezhejiang.gov.cn/2016-08/03/c_557810.htm)
   supplies photographs and describes the gourd-shaped towers and triangular
   arrangement. The miniature preserves three towers and the arrangement, but
   exaggerates their size relative to the island and does not claim exact distances.
3. [Hangzhou tourism bureau — Moon-Viewing on the Mid-Autumn Festival](https://wgly.hangzhou.gov.cn/art/2023/8/23/art_1229733751_58948820.html)
   describes Lesser Yingzhou's lake-within-an-island garden and crossing causeways.
   Used to give the Three Pools scene its enclosing island and zigzag garden bridges.
4. [Hangzhou government — Hangzhou West Lake](https://www.ehangzhou.gov.cn/2020-04/22/c_242880.htm)
   describes the lake, hills, causeways and pagodas as a combined cultural landscape.
5. [Hangzhou.com — 带你了解杭州雷峰塔](https://z.hangzhou.com.cn/2022/syjy/content/content_8421276.html)
   provides photographs of the rebuilt tower and identifies copper roof tiles,
   brackets and railings. Used for dark red structural members, layered eaves and
   subdued copper/gold roof trim, rather than a generic conical pagoda.
6. [Chung Tai Shan Museum contribution — 雷峰塔遗址寻踪](https://www.ctworld.org.tw/buddaart/08-gb.htm)
   identifies the historical five-story octagonal form and explains the modern
   reconstruction over the archaeological remains. The current miniature follows
   the photographed five-tier octagonal silhouette, not the ruined historical tower.

Reference caution: the Hangzhou tourism bureau's 2022 page and its English
translation reverse the number of sides and stories in one sentence. That
sentence was not used as a geometry requirement. The independently described
octagonal form and photographed five visible roof tiers guide this scene.

Artistic changes: surrounding paths, vegetation, boulders and small boats are
generic scenic composition details; hill profiles and relative placements are
compressed. The five apertures are modeled as dark round insets with raised rims,
not through-cut interior cavities. These scenes should not be presented as
architectural documentation.
